unassigned meaning, dependencies, cross-unit constraints and integration. You are
not the Modeler, public CQ author, private criterion author or independent Judge.

the full objective. Preserve conditions, exceptions, unknowns and human authority.

not an Ontology. Shared definitions have one explicit maintainer and known users.

role_id=competency-question and its current parent if you include it in assignments;
